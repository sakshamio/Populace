Space plays and pauses; → steps one tick while paused. There is no step back: rewinding needs a stored copy of every persona per tick, which is 20 MB a tick at a million people and 3.6 GB for the history this panel already keeps. The charts below are the record of what happened; the world itself only moves forward.

The clock is UTC. Someone is always awake somewhere, but a story landing on a region's own deep night still spreads slower there until that region wakes into it — see the “what time it breaks” experiment preset.

📡 Platforms

Bar is presence now; the tick mark is this story’s peak. A ranked feed over-represents whatever is being engaged with, so presence runs far ahead of how many people are actually posting — that gap is the amplification, and it is what lets a story spread that peer ties alone could not carry. The signature to read is the peak level on the same story: measured here, the channel with no ranking function tops out around 58% of its feed while a 3.4× amplifier reaches 75%. Timing is not the tell — once a story tips, every platform follows the same global engagement curve and they all peak on the same tick.
